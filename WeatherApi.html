Punjab


Punjab province of Pakistan is home to millions of people and some of
its most famous cities include Lahore, Faisalabad, Rawalpindi,
Multan, Sialkot and Bahawalpur.


Sindth


Sindh, the vibrant province of Pakistan, embraces iconic cities such as
Karachi, Hyderabad, and Larkana, each carrying its own story.


Khyber Pakhtunkhwa


Khyber Pakhtunkhwa province of Pakistan is known for its scenic beauty
and vibrant culture, with famous cities like Peshawar, Mardan,
Abbottabad, Swat, Kohat, and Dera Ismail Khan.


Balochistan


Balochistan province of Pakistan, the largest by area, includes renowned
cities such as Quetta, Khuzdar, Gwadar, Turbat, Sibi, and Chaman.
